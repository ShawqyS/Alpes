The following options of liquid treatment can be combined to create the perfect product profile. Most treatments come in clear, medium and dark applications. Custom formulas are also available to meet your exact needs.

## Smoking Options:
- Hardwood
- Applewood
- Cherrywood
- American Walnut
- Mezquite
- Liquid Smoke

## Colorants:
- Colorants for meat (no flavor)
- Caramel
- High adherence smoke

## Flavoring:
- Honey
- Clavos de olor
- Maple
- Brown Sugar
- Paprika
- Garlic
- Pineapple
- Black Pepper
- Red Pepper
- Roast Beef

## De-Moldants:
- Acidic and non-acidic de-moldants come in regular and strong applications.
