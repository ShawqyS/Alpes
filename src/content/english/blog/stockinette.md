The innovations have been applied to the traditional point mesh. You can enjoy many of the same labor reductions, performance increases, improved product color and performance increases with standard point mesh applications.

- Material 100 % polyester.
- Easy to peel with pre-treatment with de-mold agent.
- Reduce smoking time with optional liquid smoking pre-treatment. The pre-treatment also improves the consistency of the color, while saving labor and materials needed to clean the smokers.
- Increase performance with precorrugated point mesh "in the shape of a ring" that fits directly to most standard stuffing horns. The continuous stuffing system uses a double stapler to reduce labor and material costs.
