We offer mesh made of tight polyester fabric in a variety of elasticities and patterns. This allows customers to create an exclusive look for their products. The mesh range also includes casings with specific features, such as easy peeling.

In addition, these meshes are available with added value transfer capabilities. These allow you to apply a surface structure, smoked color and/or flavoring in a single operation.

## Features:
- Superior peeling capacity since its thin coating acts as a buffer for protein exudates, preventing the casing from adhering to the meat surface. This is the premium solution for eliminating holes, cracks and blisters on the surface.

- Improve the total peeling performance by 37% through labor savings and peeling capacity. In short: maximize your plant capacity by increasing your yield.

- The uniform and added value appearance is guaranteed with the protective coating barrier that produces a high value shine comparable to the expensive collagen films.

- The products finished with a softer crust are the distinctive seal of these casings that no collagen film can produce. A softer crust equals a higher consumer acceptance.

- Better yields by preventing protein exudates from penetrating the mesh, resulting in higher cooking yields.

- Reduce material waste by 20% through casings with the pre-stretched technology of the Specialty Meshes, which allows a stuffing with less casing needed for tying.

- Maximize existing equipment by expanding the range of products that can be produced with the Specialty Meshes. Designed for high-production slicers/tierers, allowing the complete automation of processes with traditionally labor-intensive labor.

- Hundreds of options by combining the patterns, flavors and colors of casings to match the exact specifications of your product.
